What it does

VIMS is one system with two halves: a local-first desktop workspace for AI, agents, and code, and a set of browser apps built on peer-to-peer primitives and a shared identity vault. Every browser app also runs inside the desktop client.

01
One identityA Nostr key you generate and hold, shared across every surface through keys.vims.com.
02
Local by defaultDesktop apps run on your hardware; use Ollama models or bring your own API keys.
03
Peer-to-peer dataBrowser apps replicate directly between your devices and collaborators — no VIMS server holds your files.
04
Agent-readyEvery app is reachable by agents through MCP, Flow, and the SDK.
05
No account requiredShared rooms open from a key alone; collaborators do not sign up.
06
Free to startThe desktop app and browser surfaces are free. Bookkeeper is the one paid app.

FAQ

Do I need the desktop app to use the browser apps?

No. Every browser surface works on its own at its subdomain. The desktop client adds local AI, agents, and the developer tools.

Which apps are free?

The desktop app and all browser surfaces are free. Bookkeeper is licensed separately.

How does VIMS Apps connect to the rest of VIMS?

It shares your identity from keys.vims.com and is reachable by agents through MCP and Flow, so it works alongside every other VIMS app.
